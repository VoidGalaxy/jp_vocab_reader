# Home V10.1 Direction

V10.1 keeps the approved materials and lighting, while correcting the visual
hierarchy before code is written.

- The forest-green notebook is the dominant object and exposes more cover.
- The title note is smaller and quieter, so its copy reads as an invitation
  rather than the scene's main mass.
- The CTA is a compact ticket attached to the note, not a second banner.
- Yellow, coral, and blue tabs sit in the notebook's lower-right third as
  physical index tabs, with visible desk gaps and individual contact shadows.
- Shiori is smaller and tucked into the note tape, making her a reading charm
  rather than an independent mascot.
- Desktop and mobile use separate compositions. Both remain opaque full scenes
  with no text baked into their safe zones.

Do not replace V10 production assets until this direction is explicitly
approved. If approved, create a fresh coordinate contract from these images;
do not reuse V10 anchor values.
