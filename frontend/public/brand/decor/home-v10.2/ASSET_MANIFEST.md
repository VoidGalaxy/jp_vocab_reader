# Home V10.2 Prop Scene

This production scene is wired into `HomeDashboard.tsx`; `globals.css`
and the V10.1 source files remain unchanged. This phase composites three already-approved
transparent desk props (`phase131/`) onto the untouched V10.1 base scene
photos to produce two full-scene PNGs, without moving, resizing,
or retouching the book/note/CTA/tabs/Shiori that V10.1 already locked in.

## Source

- Base desktop: `frontend/public/brand/decor/home-v10.1/home-v10.1-scene-desktop.png` (1672x940)
- Base mobile: `frontend/public/brand/decor/home-v10.1/home-v10.1-scene-mobile.png` (941x1672)
- Props (all from `frontend/public/brand/decor/phase131/`, transparent WebP with real
  per-pixel alpha and a baked-in soft studio shadow -- see that folder's own
  `ASSET_MANIFEST.md`):
  - `desk-prop-washi-tape.webp` (native 400x353)
  - `desk-prop-pen.webp` (native 184x400)
  - `desk-prop-paperclip.webp` (native 400x378)

`frontend/public/brand/decor/home-v3/home-v3-prop-*.png` was **not** used --
those carry a dark rectangular mat and stray background remnants from the
old V3 desk-surface shoot, which is exactly the "black box / halo" failure
mode this gate exists to avoid.

Pen is not used on mobile (per spec). Each prop was resized directly to its

Pre-composite region checks confirmed every placement lands on bare desk:
paperclip and pen sit clear of the book's right edge and ribbon (pen has a
~70px gap to the book's right edge, well over the 45px minimum), and
paperclip/washi tape do not touch the book, ribbon, or index tabs at either
breakpoint.

## Color treatment

None. Sampled local-desk-average tint blending was implemented but left at
`tint_strength = 0` for the final candidates -- side-by-side inspection
showed the props' existing gold/sage/kraft tones already read naturally
against this desk's warm wood without any correction, so no global or
per-prop color shift was applied (keeping with "매우 제한적으로" and
"필요할 때만").

## Shadow treatment

The washi tape roll's own baked-in studio shadow was visible but faint at
its placed size; the paperclip and pen (thin/small silhouettes) read as
flat cutouts with no ground contact once placed on this scene, which is
the one issue this phase's single allowed self-correction pass fixed.

All three desktop props and both mobile props received the same two-layer,
alpha-derived warm-brown (`rgb(61,40,18)`) ground shadow, generated by
blurring and offsetting each prop's own resized alpha channel (so the
shadow is exactly the prop's silhouette -- ring for the paperclip, tapered
body for the pen, roll outline for the washi tape -- never a rectangle):

- contact: offset (3px, 5px), Gaussian blur 3px, opacity 0.22
- ambient: offset (7px, 10px), Gaussian blur 9px, opacity 0.10

Offset direction (right/down) matches this scene's existing light source
(the plant's cast-shadow diagonals and the book's own drop shadow both fall
down-and-right), so the added shadows read as consistent with the photo's
existing lighting rather than a second light source. Book and tab baked
shadows were not touched.

## Pixel-diff verification against V10.1

Diffing each candidate against its V10.1 source (tolerance >6/255 to allow
for PNG re-encode rounding) confirms changes are confined to the prop
regions:

- Desktop: 25,023 changed px out of 1,571,680 (1.6%), all within the three
  prop+shadow footprints. Sampled book/note/CTA/tab region: max diff 3
  (encode noise only, no shape/position change).
- Mobile: 23,759 changed px out of 1,573,352 (1.5%). Sampled book/note
  region: 0 diff.

## Preview methodology

Previews are not simple downscales. They replicate the exact `.home-v10-scene`
/ `.home-v10-scene-art` cover/center crop math from `frontend/app/globals.css`
(read only, not modified):

- container size: viewport width x (`100svh - navOffset`) --
  navOffset 64px at >=768px, 70px below that
- art box width: `max(viewportWidth, aspectPct * viewportHeight/100 - offsetPx)`
  -- desktop `aspectPct=177.87, offsetPx=113.84`; mobile `aspectPct=56.28,
  offsetPx=39.4` -- height derived from the source image's own aspect ratio
  (matches `object-fit: fill` onto a same-ratio box, i.e. uniform scale)
- art box centered over the container; container clips anything outside it

Assumed viewport heights (not specified by the CSS, chosen as representative
device/browser heights): 1080px at 1920 width, 800px at 1280 width, 844px at
390 width (iPhone 12/13/14-class safe-area viewport).

## Breakpoint judgment

- **1920**: all three props visible, framing the scene at its corners
  without touching the book/note/CTA/tabs/Shiori. PASS.
- **1280x960 live QA**: book/note/CTA/tabs/Shiori fully intact and unmoved;
  washi tape remains partially visible at bottom-left, while paperclip and
  pen are cropped out by the taller viewport's cover calculation. This is
  allowed by the composition contract; the center UI was not shifted to
  preserve peripheral props. PASS.
- **390**: only the paperclip (top-right) and washi tape (bottom-left) are
  visible, framing the scene without adding clutter; pen is absent (mobile
  never places it). PASS.

## Mat / halo / rectangular-shadow check

Visual inspection of each prop at native composite resolution (before and
after the shadow pass) and a diff-only render (pixel differences amplified
8x) show the shadow silhouette tracking each prop's own alpha shape (ring
outline for the paperclip, tapered body outline for the pen) with no
straight edges. No dark rectangular mat or bright halo is present around
any of the three props at either breakpoint. PASS.

## Integration QA and remaining risks

- Chrome/CDP live renders passed at 1920x960, 1280x960, 390x844, 1024,
  375, and 320 with no horizontal overflow or image 404s. CTA, sample,
  vocabulary, review, and deck actions retained their existing behavior.
- At 1280x960 the paperclip and pen are outside the crop. They remain
  visible at 1920 and are intentionally peripheral rather than reasons to
  move or shrink the primary notebook composition.
- The paperclip and pen synthetic shadows are necessarily faint (their
  silhouettes are mostly thin outlines), so grounding is subtle by
  construction -- acceptable at review size, but worth a second look at
  100% zoom if a future phase revisits shadow strength.
- No further color-matching pass was applied after the approved correction
  round. Any future tint change is a new phase, not an integration fix.
